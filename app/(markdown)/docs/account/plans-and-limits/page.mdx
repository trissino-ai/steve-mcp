What you can do through Steve MCP depends on your Steve plan. Steve does not charge per question. It limits how much you can do per hour and per day.

## The MCP plan

The MCP plan is built for using Steve inside your assistant. It costs **$49 per month**.

<Facts>
  <Fact label="People">1 user</Fact>
  <Fact label="Projects">1 project</Fact>
  <Fact label="Competitors">3 competitors</Fact>
  <Fact label="Included">Competitor news, intelligence canvas, LinkedIn, X and Instagram activity, Google and Meta ad tracking</Fact>
  <Fact label="Not included">Battle cards, website tracking, review sites, reports</Fact>
  <Fact label="Refresh">Weekly, not daily</Fact>
</Facts>

Other plans have other limits, such as more competitors. Your organization's plan decides what you get. See Steve's [pricing page](https://app.hiresteve.ai/payments).

## Trial

- The MCP plan trial lasts **3 days** with no card. Other plans start with 14 days.
- Steve warns you about the days left while you are on a trial.
- When the trial ends, Steve **blocks everything that saves or starts something**. Reading still works.
- You see: "Hey, your trial has expired. Please subscribe to use further." Subscribe at the Steve payments page, then try again.
- Drafting steps also count as saving here, so they stop too.

## When a feature is not in your plan

Some tools need a feature your plan may not have.

| Feature | Tools affected | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| Battle cards | The six battle card tools | A "not enabled for your organization" message |
| Website tracking | The six website tracking tools | The same message. Reading website changes just returns empty. |
| Google or Meta ads | The ads tools | A short note that the source is not enabled. Not an error. |
| Reports | Report tools | Usually no templates to run |
| Review sites | Reviews | Usually empty |

Your assistant is told not to retry. On a self-serve plan, upgrade on the payments page. On a managed plan, ask whoever manages your Steve subscription.

<Callout type="note" title="Plans can be custom">
  Some organizations are set up by hand. Your list can differ. If a tool says it is not enabled, ask your Steve contact.
</Callout>

## Plan caps

You can hit a cap on projects, competitors or tracked social accounts. Steve says `limit_reached`. Remove something or upgrade.

## Rate limits

Limits reset at the start of each hour (UTC).

| Limit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Reading (news, lists, status) | 300 per hour |
| Writing (saving, starting jobs) | 60 per hour |
| Reports | 5 starts per hour. Each template 3 per day. |
| Canvases | 5 builds per hour |
| Battle card rewrites | 10 per hour |
| Competitor searches | 5 per hour, and 1 per day for each project |

- Drafting steps (the "prepare" tools) use the **write** limit, except the report check, which uses the read limit.
- The limit counts per connection **and** per person across all connections. A new connection does not reset it.
- Runs that were refused or never started do not count against the daily caps.
- When you hit a limit, Steve says when it resets.
