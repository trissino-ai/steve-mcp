You sign in once, in the browser. Your assistant never sees your password.

## The Approve page

When you add Steve, your assistant opens a Steve page titled **Approve this connection**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">If you are signed out, you see **Sign in to continue**. Sign in to Steve. You return to the same page.</Step>
  <Step title="Check the application">The page shows which application asks to use Steve, and where Steve will send you after you approve.</Step>
  <Step title="Pick an organization">Under **Select organization**, choose the one to connect. The page says **Connecting as** and the organization name.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve or deny">Click **Approve**, or **Deny** to cancel. After Approve, you can close the tab.</Step>
</Steps>

  The application name and logo are set by whoever registered the application. Steve cannot check them. Approve only if you started this connection yourself.

## What you are allowing

The page says: the application can **read and write this organization's Steve data as you, until you revoke it**.

- It acts as you. It can do what you can do in that organization.
- Steve lists two scopes, `steve:read` and `steve:write`. Steve does not enforce them. **Do not rely on a read-only connection.**
- Generating and sharing canvases is only for organization managers, the same as in the Steve app.
- No Steve tool deletes projects or competitors.

## One person, one organization

A connection is one person in one organization. You choose the organization at approval. It is fixed after that.

- To use another organization, connect again and pick the other one.
- If you are not in any organization, the page tells you to create or join one first.
- Any signed-in member can approve a connection.
- If you leave the organization, the connection stops working.
- Organizations of the Signals type cannot connect.
- Projects are not part of the connection. Your assistant picks the project in each request.

## How long sign-in lasts

| What | How long | In plain words |
|---|---|---|
| Access | 1 hour | Your assistant renews it by itself. |
| Renewal | 60 days | Each renewal gives a new one. If you do not use Steve for 60 days, connect again. |

- If an old renewal key is used a second time, Steve cancels the whole connection to keep you safe. Connect again.
- When Steve renews, it checks you are still in the organization.

## Revoke a connection

Revoke in Steve. Do this even if you also remove the connector in your assistant.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Approve page">Add Steve again in your assistant, or open the Steve sign-in page from it. The page lists **Existing connections**.</Step>
  <Step title="Find the connection">Each row shows when it was connected and when it was last used.</Step>
  <Step title="Click Revoke">The connection stops working right away. You only see and revoke connections you made yourself in that organization.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Removing it in your assistant">
  We have not confirmed that removing the connector in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or VS Code also revokes it in Steve. Revoke in Steve to be sure.
</Callout>
