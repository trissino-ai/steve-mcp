See what your competitors are advertising. Google ads come from the Google Ads Transparency Center. Meta ads come from the Meta Ad Library: Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Threads.

Steve can only show ads once it knows the competitor's Google advertiser or Facebook page. If it is missing, the assistant asks you to supply it. See [Competitors](/docs/tools/competitors).

## Try asking

<Prompt>What Google ads is Klue running?</Prompt>
<Prompt>Show Meta ads from my competitors on Instagram.</Prompt>
<Prompt>Any video ads from Crayon this month?</Prompt>
<Prompt>Find ads that mention "free trial".</Prompt>

## Get Google ads

<ToolBadge name="get_google_ads" bucket="read" />

Ads from the Google Ads Transparency Center.

<Facts>
  <Fact label="You can say">Which project. A competitor (optional). Date range. Format: text, image or video. Words to search in the ad.</Fact>
  <Fact label="Paging">12 ads per page by default, up to 48. Ask for the next page to see more.</Fact>
  <Fact label="You get">Title, body, button text, link, preview, format, whether it is still running, start and end date, and the competitor. Plus the total.</Fact>
</Facts>

## Get Meta ads

<ToolBadge name="get_meta_ads" bucket="read" />

Ads from the Meta Ad Library.

<Facts>
  <Fact label="You can say">Same as Google, plus carousel as a format. You can also pick where ads show: Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Threads or Audience Network.</Fact>
  <Fact label="You get">The same fields as Google, plus where each ad is shown.</Fact>
</Facts>

<Callout type="note" title="Plan">
  If your plan does not include Google or Meta ad tracking, you get a plain message saying so, not an error. The entry MCP plan includes both.
</Callout>
