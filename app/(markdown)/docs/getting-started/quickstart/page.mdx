<Meta eyebrow="Getting started" {...metadata} />

This guide uses Claude. For other assistants, see the [connection guides](/docs/connect/claude).

<Callout type="note" title="Before you start">
  You need a Steve account and a paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise). On Team or Enterprise, an owner must turn on custom connectors first.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connectors">In the Claude desktop app, go to **Settings → Connectors**. On claude.ai, go to **Settings → Customize → Connectors**.</Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">Click **Add custom connector**. Give it any name, for example Steve.</Step>
  <Step title="Paste the address">Paste `https://app.hiresteve.ai/api/agent/mcp` as the remote MCP server. Leave **Client ID** and **Client Secret** empty.</Step>
  <Step title="Accept the warning">Claude says it is an unverified third-party connector. That is normal for any connector Anthropic did not build.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve in Steve">A browser window opens. Sign in to Steve if asked. Pick your organization and click **Approve**.</Step>
  <Step title="Allow the tools">Claude asks permission for each tool. Choose **Always allow** the first time, so you do not approve 47 tools one by one.</Step>
</Steps>

## Ask your first question

If you have no project yet, start here:

<Prompt>Use Steve to start tracking my competitors. My company is acme.com.</Prompt>

Steve reads your website and drafts a project. It shows you the draft. Check it, then say yes. Then Steve looks for your competitors and shows you a list to correct.

<Callout type="tip" title="Give it a few minutes">
  New competitors are researched in the background. News appears first. Other sources can take minutes to hours.
</Callout>

When competitors exist, try:

<Prompt>What is the latest news on my competitors?</Prompt>
<Prompt>Who am I tracking in Steve?</Prompt>
<Prompt>Show me the Meta ads my competitors are running.</Prompt>
