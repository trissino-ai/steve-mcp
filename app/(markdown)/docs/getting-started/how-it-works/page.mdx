Steve tells your assistant how to behave when it connects. This page explains those rules in plain words.

## Answers come from collected data

Your assistant must not answer about a competitor from its own memory. It calls a Steve tool. If Steve has not collected the data yet, the assistant says so.

Every item has a **source link** and a **date**. Your assistant should show both. If it does not, ask: "Where is this from?"

<Prompt>What is the latest news on Klue? Include the source and date for each item.</Prompt>

## Suggested next steps

After most answers, Steve suggests up to three next steps. They are written as questions. Your assistant asks you the first one and waits.

It never runs a suggested step on its own. You stay in control.

## Draft, confirm, save

When something will be saved, Steve works in two steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare">Steve reads a website or checks a request and shows a draft. Nothing is saved.</Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">You read the draft, fix anything, and say yes.</Step>
  <Step title="Write">Only now does the assistant save the project, competitors, tracker or report.</Step>
</Steps>

This applies to creating a project, adding competitors, tracking website pages and running a report.

<Callout type="note" title="A convention, not a lock">
  The draft step is how your assistant is told to work. Steve's server does not force it. Read what your assistant is about to save.
</Callout>

## What an empty result means

An empty list is not always "nothing happened". Steve gives a reason. The usual ones:

| Reason | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Still collecting | Steve has not finished the first collection. | Wait and ask again. |
| Nothing found | Steve looked and found nothing for this filter or date range. | Widen the dates or filters. |
| Not tracked | You do not track this competitor or account yet. | Ask Steve to start tracking it. |
| Not in your plan | Your plan does not include this source. | See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits). |

## Collection takes time

Steve does not look at the web live. It collects on a schedule.

- After you add a competitor, the first data arrives from **a few minutes to a few hours** later.
- News usually fills first.
- On the MCP plan, data refreshes weekly, not daily.
- Long jobs (reports, canvases, competitor searches) run in the background. Your assistant starts them, then checks the status later.
