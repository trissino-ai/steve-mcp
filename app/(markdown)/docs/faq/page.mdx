## Which assistants work?

Claude (desktop and web), Claude Code, ChatGPT, Cursor and VS Code. Steve's own guides cover Claude and Claude Code. The others use their own current steps. See [Connect your assistant](/docs/connect/claude).

## What is the address?

`https://app.hiresteve.ai/api/agent/mcp`. It uses MCP Streamable HTTP.

## Do I need an API key?

No. You sign in with your Steve account. Leave any client ID and secret empty.

## Is the connection read-only?

No. Your assistant can read and write your organization's Steve data as you. Steve lists read and write scopes, but does not enforce them.

## Can I use it if my organization is a Signals organization?

No. Signals organizations cannot connect.

## Why is my answer empty?

Steve may still be collecting, may have found nothing, may not track that competitor, or your plan may not include the source. See [How Steve answers](/docs/getting-started/how-it-works).

## How fresh is the data?

Steve collects on a schedule, not live. New competitors fill in minutes to hours. On the MCP plan, data refreshes weekly.

## Does Steve answer from memory?

No. Your assistant is told to use Steve's collected data and to cite the source and date.

## Will my assistant publish things?

Not without asking. Reports, canvases and battle cards are private until you agree to a public link. You can take a link down at any time.

## How do I switch organizations?

Connect again and pick the other organization.

## How do I disconnect?

Revoke the connection in Steve. See [Sign-in and permissions](/docs/account/sign-in).

## What does it cost?

The MCP plan is $49 per month, with a 3-day trial. See [Plans and limits](/docs/account/plans-and-limits).
